# Later Bondora vintages as a locked historical holdout

The original profitable cases came from loans issued in 2018, revalued using the 24 May 2024 loan tape. We chose fresh cohorts and wrote these definitions after reading only the headers, dictionary and file metadata of the newer workbook, before inspecting its outcome records. The policy is fixed in commit `0fda6dd`. It selects on expected incremental money and separately predicts the chance that a losing pilot will change the next decision and earn back its loss. [Rule, endpoints and inference](pilot-rule-protocol.md)

## Two separate calendar tests

| Sample | Pilot origination months, inclusive | Continuation origination months, inclusive | Minimum seasoning after the continuation month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary, after the discovery cash cut | June–September 2024 | July–October 2025 | 12 complete months |
| Secondary, older and more seasoned | July 2020–September 2022 | August 2021–October 2023 | 36 complete months |

The primary window begins in the first full month after the 2024 historical discovery ledger. Its last month allows the latest continuation to reach one year in the November 2026 release. The secondary window begins after the last original continuation month, June 2020. Every pilot and continuation in either window therefore has a different origination date from the loans scored in the original study. The older cohort predates the discovery cash cut. Loan payments from these cohorts had not been evaluated for this rule. This is a temporal holdout under the same originating platform, retrospectively evaluated in November 2026. Both windows stay in the report regardless of observed performance. Four calendar months in the primary window span only two quarters, too few for the planned four-quarter block interval.

## What is observable when

Read the 4 November 2026 public loan workbook, 139,650,301 bytes, SHA-256 `463277de6f9c2aae2a726b8064bd8d36682222bc02424a30321c824fd2ef763f`. Its worksheet dictionary defines `customer_risk_rating` at issue and `has_default_within_12_months` as the measured first-year default flag. Use issue times as supplied, with no timezone conversion. The provider supplies no timezone in this field. The original new/returning indicator is absent. Form groups by country and the grade at issue, retaining missing grades in their own group. The grade defines which loans inform one another. The numerical policy and its cutoff are identical across groups. [Provider statistics and official download](https://goandgrow.eu/en/public-statistics/) · [Workbook](https://sabanners001.blob.core.windows.net/statistics/public/loan_dataset_investor.xlsx)

For each nonempty group and origin month s, the first funded loan is the pilot. Order by the reported origination time, then a fixed SHA-256 key of country, rating, origination time, issued amount, initial interest rate and initial duration. Break any ties remaining on all these fields by workbook order, a documented proxy for intraday order. Do not use current status, eventual default, cash, projected NPV or remaining balance to order loans. Use the first up to 25 ordered loans in month s+13 as the continuation. Keep zero and short continuations. Groups require 50 measured historical loans, with initial risk q estimated from the mature origin months s−24 through s−13 inclusive and the Jeffreys half-count in the previous implementation. The ordinary refresh uses the measured outcomes of s−12 through s−1 and defaults to the initial q when no labels are available. Both arms see this refresh before making the continuation decision, and neither uses outcomes from the candidate’s own origin month for this update.

At issue, the committed rule receives the actual count and face value of the first up to 25 loans from each of the preceding 12 complete months in the same group, including zeros. Its public-update prior pools all eligible-for-estimation origin/update pairs across group-months in this same archive with at least 50 measured historical loans and an update date at or before that origin decision. Build these historical pairs from every available earlier month. No initial negative-margin screen applies to the historical pairs. No payment totals or eventual repayment flags enter selection.

## Comparable amounts and missing data

Follow both actions on the same subsequent funded portfolio. The scenario pays +10% of actual principal for no first-year default or −45% for a first-year default. For the primary cash measurement sum the loan’s recorded principal, interest, extra interest and late fee payments, then deduct the actual issued amount. Exclude the separately reported servicing/maintenance fee. Report outstanding principal separately for interpretation. These totals share one workbook cutoff and are undiscounted. They can grow after the cut. The old and new rules face the same pilots, follow-up actions and matured signals. Raw borrower records and any internal row identifiers stay outside the public outputs.

Missing mature labels are unknown. Historical risk counts use measured labels only. Never replace an unknown first-year default with zero. A missing pilot signal prevents evaluating the post-signal action. A missing continuation label prevents scoring that portfolio’s binary margin when the action changes. Unavailable cash components make that position’s cash unknown. Publish the corresponding completeness counts. Do not drop an issued candidate from the predicted-policy denominator because of any future outcome. If any required realized margin is unknown, report coverage and avoid a claim of confirmed gain for a partially observed population. For a common decision at follow-up, the incremental portfolio margin is exactly zero regardless of its later measurements.

The archive URL rotates. The digest verifies exact bytes and guards against scoring a replacement workbook as the same study. The published episode-level paired outcomes allow evaluating the frozen policies independently of availability of the historical binary.
