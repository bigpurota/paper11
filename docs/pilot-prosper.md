# Did the pilot rule transfer to Prosper?

The US lender supplied 30 eligible opportunities. Both the new and the original rule chose to skip all 30. Their incremental cash was $0 compared with skipping by definition. This check produced no evidence that the rule can pick a loss-recovering pilot in advance. The selection rule, cash endpoints and cutoffs were fixed before inspecting these Prosper results, after the two Bondora tests. The broader Prosper archive had already been used for other risk-scoring experiments in the project. ## From listings to decisions

The March 2014 export contains 113,937 listings. Five canceled listings were excluded. Collapsing 871 extra listings from 827 repeated loan keys left 113,061 funded loans. All repeated records agreed on origination, segmentation, default and every cash component. The January 2008–January 2012 pilot window contains 31,947 distinct loans in 338 nonempty US region × new/returning customer × month groups. Of these, 293 had at least 50 measured historical loans and 30 also had negative predicted direct margins. Repayment and cash were known for all 30 matched pilot comparisons. The archive contained 182 funded loans for which a recorded default could not be timed from its cycle number. Historical rate estimates exclude those labels. [Coverage and timing](../results/pilot_prosper_cohort.csv)

Forcing a pilot in every eligible episode gave a total cash difference of −$11,962.08. Three pilot signals changed the funding instruction, each in a follow-up month with zero borrowers in that segment. Thus **zero actual continuation decisions changed** and **zero strict cash recoveries occurred**. The expected profitability score and the strict-recovery probability cannot be assessed for discrimination between realized strict recoveries and non-recoveries in a sample with no positive events. Dollar figures are cumulative gross cash through the common extraction date. [All paired decisions](../results/pilot_prosper_episodes.csv)

## Replication and accounting

Run `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m paper11.pilot_prosper --out results --cache data/cache`. The input is verified against SHA-256 `71c1c1931f65bc4d2e3a8f3603d865e074e327b2271590f3a06829197b9cce0f`. The source dictionary states that `LP_CustomerPayments` excludes recoveries after charge-off. To those receipts we add principal recovered (gross less net principal loss) and recovered interest and fees, then subtract the original principal. This counts each recovery component once. The result excludes servicing, collections, financing and discounting. No borrower or loan identifiers are published. [March 2014 export](https://s3.amazonaws.com/udacity-hosted-downloads/ud651/prosperLoanData.csv) · [Prosper tape dictionary](https://video.udacity-data.com/topher/2023/December/6571fe24_prosper-loan-data-variable-definitions/prosper-loan-data-variable-definitions.xlsx) · [Adapter](../src/paper11/pilot_prosper.py)

The 30 eligible episodes occurred in just four of 17 calendar quarters. Of 2,500 four-quarter block resamples, 2,249 contained at least one eligible opportunity. The two policies had identical zero recorded gains in all of those draws. This describes agreement between the specified policies in this sample. The absence of a positive strict event leaves sensitivity to future successes unknown. [Five-cohort results](../results/pilot_rule_summary.csv)
