# Data and provenance

* Assigned paper and online appendix: Kozodoi, Lessmann, Alamgir, Moreira-Matias & Papakonstantinou, [arXiv:2407.13009v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13009v1). Also published in [European Journal of Operational Research 324(2), 616–628](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377221725000839). We compare against the assigned preprint. Authors' Spanish microloan data include a randomized approval holdout and were not supplied.
* Taiwan: I-Cheng Yeh, [Default of Credit Card Clients, UCI](https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H), CC BY 4.0. 30,000 financed credit accounts, 6,636 next-month defaults. X1 is credit limit; X6–11 are six past repayment statuses, X12–17 are bills and X18–23 are payments. The decision is a hypothetical renewal at the end of September 2005. All clients share one outcome month, so calendar extrapolation is tested separately on Prosper. KFold uses no outcomes.
* United States: [Udacity-hosted historical Prosper extract](https://s3.amazonaws.com/udacity-hosted-downloads/ud651/prosperLoanData.csv), [Prosper export documentation](https://www.prosper.com/Downloads/Services/Documentation/ProsperDataExport_Details.html). The March 2014 snapshot contains 113,937 entries. The unit in our test is the borrower's first funded loan observed in the extract. The endpoint is first *recorded default cycle* in 1–12, including subsequently recovered accounts. A default/charge-off with a missing or nonpositive cycle is excluded. Cancelled listings are excluded; no full-term status filter. Origination is the index date. Through February 2013 loans have at least twelve months of follow-up. This target differs from both next-month card default and Monedo's three consecutive months of delinquency.
* Historical approval is simulated for the Taiwan and Prosper cohorts. Raw files contain borrowers the original institutions already financed. Artificially rejected cases retain their historical observed outcomes as blinded ground truth for holdout evaluation and explicitly labeled retrospective diagnostics. Neither dataset identifies the effect of lending to actual rejects. The release contains no source microdata or borrower identifiers. The new pilot studies publish episode-level cash and signal outcomes needed to audit the comparisons.
* Input download URLs and SHA-256 fingerprints are in `src/paper11/data.py` and, for the two Bondora ZIP files, `src/paper11/lender_pilot.py`. Downloads are atomic and verified on every run. The UCI archive also offers the same observations in XLS format. Observed-model features exclude identifier, protected demographics, and all post-decision events. Missing numerical predictors use fitting-cohort medians. Public results are aggregates.
* Philippines: Karlan and Zinman, Science (2011); public analytic file prepared by Athey, Fava, Karlan, Osman and Zinman. A separate score-window trial with actual offers and incomplete take-up. [Source, exchangeability, currency reconciliation and external reference counts](rct.md). The CSV is pinned to a commit and verified on download in `src/paper11/data.py`; only aggregate results are included.
* US small-business lending: the September 30, 2026 SBA 504 FOIA file for fiscal approval years 1991–2009 comes from the [SBA Open Data Portal](https://data.sba.gov/dataset/7a-504-foia). The dated file is pinned by SHA-256. We use project state and administrative dates to measure a recorded charge-off within three years of first disbursement. Only aggregate outputs enter this repository. [Sample flow, assumptions and chronological replay](pilot-replay.md)
* US SBA 7(a): the two releases covering approvals in FY1991–1999 and FY2000–2009 share the September 2026 snapshot date. File sizes, hashes, eligibility and recorded gross charge-off accounting are documented with the chronological [transfer study](pilot-transfer.md). [Official US SBA catalog](https://data.sba.gov/dataset/7a-504-foia)
* Bondora consumer loans: a versioned July 2021 portfolio export contains issued principal, dates, recorded defaults and cumulative principal, interest and penalty receipts. [Snapshot sources, linkage and limitations](lender-pilot.md) · [Bondora's explanation of portfolio reporting](https://goandgrow.eu/en/blog/understanding-detailed-borrower-loan-data-bondora/)
* Bondora temporal validation: the official 4 November 2026 workbook reports risk grade at origination, the measured first-year default flag and cumulative principal, interest, extra interest and late fees. The fixed source is 139,650,301 bytes. All dates, units, tie handling and missing-value rules accompany the [holdout protocol](pilot-holdout.md). [First-party data catalog](https://goandgrow.eu/en/public-statistics/)

## Local versus broader frequency at Wells Fargo

The SBA's 30 September 2026 7(a) fiscal 2010–2019 approval CSV (255,300,948 bytes) supplies the previously unused test vintages. Its SHA-256 is `39aa32b71fb5dbb4bfb178c9b3d597c92bb6903622027c2f8360d13e57d0abb2`. The older two 7(a) extracts supply prior disbursement histories. We use FDIC certificate 3511, project state and eligible first disbursement dates. Only aggregate outputs are published.
