This protocol is public before we compute or view new episode outcomes and paired results. The preceding 48 portfolios suggested anchoring forecasts to the historical default rate. Their diagnostic decomposition and model-input experiments are retrospective. The following test on the same pinned Bondora archive was designed with its dates, coverage metadata and earlier published results available.

## A frequency forecast with three possible adjustments

At issue q is the historical country default frequency (defaults + ½)/(measured loans + 1), based on s−24 through s−13 and at least 50 measured flags. At s+13 both strategies receive the ordinary updated frequency u from s−12 through s−1. With shared cohort records also let n be the number of measured peers and d their defaults, always excluding the selected pilot. Pilot flag Y is its first-year default. The target z is the defaulted share of original principal of the subsequent book.

Fit the following slopes sequentially on the same complete training episodes. Each scalar fit through the origin has slope Σ(x·residual)/(1+Σx²), using penalty 1 on the unstandardized probability scale. Fix all coefficients after training. The first two slopes are constrained to [0,1].

1. Start from q. Learn a on x=u−q and residual z−q. The new public forecast is P=q+a(u−q).
2. Learn b on x=(d−nP)/(20+n) and residual z−P. The peer-informed forecast is C=P+b(d−nP)/(20+n).
3. Use π=(d+20u)/(20+n) for the chance the pilot defaults. Learn signed γ on x=Y−π and residual z−C. For a test episode truncate γ only as needed for both branches C+γ(0−π), C+γ(1−π) to lie in [0,1]. Their average with probabilities (1−π,π) is exactly C. Hide or reveal Y in the same fit. In the no-peer sensitivity set n=d=0 throughout, use the same ordinary update and fit the private slope for this information set.

Publish q, u, and the direct smoothed peer rate π as simple comparators. Refit the previous boosting specification with the same June 2024 training cutoff. Also transport the frozen October 2022 boosting specification using only training outcomes that were available at its original cutoff. Hold all tuning values, loss, preprocessing, unit of observation and economic terms fixed. [Boosting specification](pilot-signal-ml-protocol.md)

## Issuing a pilot and deciding what to do next

Price an unknown signal using only already mature training analogs. Use all same-country analogs when at least 12 exist and otherwise use all training rows. For each analog, transfer its ordinary update and observed peer default proportion to the current country risk using the common shift logit(q)−logit(q_analog). Preserve endpoints 0 and 1. With no measured peers substitute the ordinary rate and retain peer count zero. Retain each analog's peer count and calculate its implied peer defaults, common probability and both conditional branches. The mean of their decision-value gains is multiplied by the issue-time expected book principal from the previous 12 months including empty ones. The gross gain per future euro equals the average optimally chosen margin with Y minus the optimal margin at C. Margin is 0.10−0.55p. No future peer records or realized pilot outcome enter the funding choice.

Use the prior direct-loss eligibility screen on q. Fund a pilot for information when its own expected direct margin is negative and its predicted direct plus incremental follow-up margin is positive. Use the preceding per-unit tolerance of 1e−12 and decline at a tie. Retain empty books with zero changed actions. Compare q→P→C for actions due to common news, and C→revealed for the single pilot. Count changes only on actual nonempty books. Paired replay cash for a changed follow-up equals signed book cash, with the pilot's cash added only when issued. Report the changes in action, signed gross ledger cash and the +10%/−45% first-year scenario individually. Against skipping all information pilots hold the common continuation decision fixed. Compare candidate selection with the legacy amount-aware selector on the same downstream anchored actions. Separately compare the first-stage frequency-only and revealed-pilot continuation decisions across the complete book opportunity set; the latter assumes disclosure and is not an achievable cash gain without paying for the pilot. Real approvals, institutional access to peers and eventual final returns are unknown.

## Readout

Primary rank and information setting are first loan and shared peers. On complete, nonempty, matched portfolios compute exposure-weighted individual Brier loss within each book, then average equally across episodes. Positive loss reduction is improvement. The two primary descriptive contrasts are the baseline versus learned common update (q minus C in loss), and the value of the pilot conditional on common records (C minus revealed in loss). Show q, u, raw smoothed peer rate, P, C, seen and boosting losses together; show country-level counts. For incompletely labeled portfolios bound the paired contrasts by assigning all unresolved principal first to nondefault and then to default and taking the extrema per book. If a pilot flag is unknown consider both branches. Give identified bounds on fixed-scenario follow-up economics when labels are incomplete and report the cash coverage independently.

Four origin months span two quarters, too few for the previous four-quarter uncertainty calculation. Report each month, country diagnostics and sensitivity to removing each month, without confidence intervals or significance claims. We will publish unfavorable comparisons, unavailable cases and any departures from these rules. These results evaluate a historical replay across funded loans.
