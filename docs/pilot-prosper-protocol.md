# Frozen cross-lender check on Prosper

This supplementary transfer test was specified after learning the two Bondora holdout results. We have used this US lender's 2014 export to study reject inference and quarterly risk forecasts. We have not computed pilot recommendations, pilot-driven changes in subsequent action or episode cash gains on these data. The archive is therefore familiar and the paired pilot endpoints are new. Keep the two original Bondora tests and their conclusions in the report.

Use the unchanged rule at commit `0fda6dd`: past twelve months of first-25 portfolio exposure, the existing mixed forecast of ordinary risk updates, effective prior strength 20, +10% / −45% decision payoffs, and a 13-month gap. The strict recovery endpoint requires a negative direct recorded cash margin, a changed follow-up action, and positive total incremental cash. Use the previously frozen cash comparisons and rank metrics, calendar-quarter blocks and success criteria. Report dollars independently of euros. Do not pick dates, risk cutoffs or segments based on realized performance.

* Population: funded Prosper loans with positive original amount and a known issue date in the pinned March 2014 tape. Drop canceled listings. Use one record per LoanKey, since the same funded loan can appear on multiple listings. Sort by LoanOriginationDate then LoanNumber, and use file order to resolve any residual ties. Check the duplicate records for consistency in the event, issued principal and cash components. Prior borrowing is known at issue: TotalProsperLoans > 0 means returning; zero or missing means new. Group by that status and the four Census regions already implemented in the project, with unknown state a separate group. A borrower may have several distinct loans.
* Evaluate every available origination month January 2008 through January 2012 inclusive. This starts after two years of archive history and ends in time for first-year outcomes of the February 2013 continuation to mature by March 2014. For each group use at least 50 measured historical flags at s−24 through s−13. Use s−12 through s−1 for the shared refresh. The first funded loan is the pilot; the first up to 25 loans in s+13 are the continuation. Empty continuation portfolios are zero exposure.
* First-year signal: a recorded first default in payment cycles 1–12, allowing later recoveries. A reported default or charge-off with missing or nonpositive cycle has an unknown signal. Other mature loans with no first recorded default are coded zero. Preserve every issued candidate in the evaluation even if its signal later proves unavailable.
* Gross cash at the common March 2014 extraction: pre-charge-off cumulative LP_CustomerPayments, plus recovered principal (LP_GrossPrincipalLoss minus LP_NetPrincipalLoss), plus LP_NonPrincipalRecoverypayments, less LoanOriginalAmount. These fields account for pre-charge-off payments and the two recovery components once each. A missing required cash field means unknown. Exclude servicing fees, collection costs, funding costs and discounting to retain the gross borrower-cash basis of the Bondora test. Later receipts are possible. Binary-scenario margins use actual principal.
* Use the analysis code and selection cutoffs fixed before the Bondora holdout; only the lender-specific source adapter changes. Record all forecastable cells, eligible size, unknown signals/cash, selected outcomes, strict recoveries and rank coverage. For insufficient or missing outcomes publish denominators and leave claims restricted to observed data. No subsequent choice between these archives establishes a new primary test.
