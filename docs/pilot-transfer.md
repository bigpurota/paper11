# Out-of-product test on SBA 7(a) loans

## Design recorded before reading 7(a) repayment outcomes

Use both September 2026 official 7(a) files for fiscal approvals 1991–2009. The calendar, four project regions, three-year recorded charge-off, twelve-quarter history, 13-quarter delay, 20-observation prior, 25 follow-up slots, chronological within-quarter draw, and decision rule are inherited from the [SBA 504 experiment](pilot-replay.md). The primary price is transferred unchanged from 504: performing margin 0.5 × 27 / (9398 − 27), charge-off margin −0.5 per unit. A prespecified second price applies the same formula to 7(a)'s own 1992–1995 disbursements. All three-year labels in that calibration period mature before the first pilot quarter. Neither price is a historical contracted 7(a) return. Exclude missing or inconsistent origination dates, recorded charge-off dates before disbursement, projects outside the four regions and incompletely matured outcomes.

At origination, select a pilot only for an initially loss-making loan with positive modeled net value. Record the full eligible denominator, the mean incremental value in selected and unselected episodes if each were piloted, and the policy value versus an updated no-pilot bank. After the pilot matures, both rules update from identical ordinary public history. Audit all outcomes including unsuitable cells and failures. No adjustment to thresholds, dates, groups or horizon is made to seek a favorable result.

For a single fixed historical replay, set seed 1109. For each region and quarter, draw a month uniformly. Beginning in this month, select one loan uniformly from the first available daily batch. In the future quarter take the first 25 funded loans in date order, randomly breaking ties within the boundary day. Use archived row order as a stable indexing convention within a date, with both file segments concatenated in chronological fiscal order. Both price scenarios, the no-pilot policy and the signal policy use the same draw. A realized success requires a previously selected pilot, a different next lending action, negative pilot margin and positive combined incremental margin on the same 25-loan panel. Report the corresponding forced-pilot count separately. The example, if present, is the earliest such episode ordered by quarter and region. The fixed draw illustrates one historical panel. Also integrate over all admissible pilot outcomes and tied future draws to quantify how often such successes would occur under the declared lottery.

Only administrative loans that actually received financing are observed. The lender's actual decision on seeing a particular repayment, the underwriting motive and whole-life realized economics are unavailable in these two tables.

Before inspecting the computed panel we also specified a cash-loss sensitivity for the one fixed draw. Hold all bank-rule decisions fixed. For each observed three-year charge-off, use its recorded gross charge-off amount divided by its gross approved principal as the scenario loss. If either field is missing for a charge-off, mark the affected scenario margin as unavailable. Give each non-event the same prespecified performing margin as in the main scenario. The equal-exposure normalization keeps the pilot and the 25 opportunities comparable. These are administrative gross charge-offs with hypothetical margins on non-events. The data do not establish the net loss retained by a bank after SBA guarantees, recoveries, funding or servicing costs.

## Population and predictive performance

The two pinned releases contain 1,027,366 approvals. Exclusions were 127,471 missing disbursement dates, 520 inconsistent approval/disbursement records, four charge-offs recorded before disbursement, 13,082 projects outside the four regions, and two loans without a fully matured three-year outcome. The 886,287 retained loans had 54,930 three-year recorded charge-offs. Every such event has an available gross charge-off fraction. The pilot-quarter pools contain 417,928 financed loans. The later-quarter pools contain 509,835. The replay uses just one pilot and 25 follow-up positions per episode. Some calendar quarters perform both roles in different episodes. [Official SBA catalog](https://data.sba.gov/dataset/7a-504-foia) · [Cohort counts](../results/pilot_transfer_cohort.csv)

At 504's frozen break-even risk of 0.2873%, all 120 first loans had negative modeled direct value. The pilot rule recommended issuing none. Forcing a pilot in all cells yielded −0.748 per episode on a principal of 100 (four-quarter moving-block 95% interval −1.264 to −0.233). The future actions with and without the signal agreed in every case.

Early 7(a) history had 3,952 events among 118,625 loans. The corresponding break-even risk was 3.3315%, performing margin 1.7232 per 100 and charge-off margin −50. The model-selected strategy, which skips 69 pilots, returned −0.598 per eligible episode versus the matched, updated no-pilot strategy. The rule assigned lower estimated value to the group with the higher observed mean. On all eligible episodes, ranking the sign of *lottery-averaged* net value by the pre-issuance predicted net had AUC 0.483. The separate fixed-draw contrast uses realized decisions and has a different target. These data provide little support for the proposed score's ability to identify profitable opportunities. Repricing with the product's earlier history changes its actions but does not establish that the banks charged these terms. ## What can be illustrated with actual repayments?

**Northeast, 1999 Q4 → 2003 Q1, fixed assignment.** Early 7(a) pricing made the first loan unprofitable in expectation. The model selected it. The archived loan drawn with seed 1109 had no three-year charge-off. After the ordinary regional update the no-pilot rule would decline. Including this one repayment switched the rule to lend. None of the 25 later loans in the fixed draw had a three-year charge-off. The incremental follow-up margin was +43.079 per 100 of pilot principal, the probe earned +1.723 and the total was +44.802. The pilot's realized payoff was positive. The other three flips among the 16 selected episodes had negative net values in the same fixed draw.

**West, 2002 Q2 → 2005 Q3, an attainable loss-recovery path.** The initial predicted default rate of 3.4353% narrowly exceeded the 3.3315% break-even threshold. The model selected a pilot. Later information common to both arms reduced the risk estimate to 2.9698% and the refreshed no-pilot rule would lend. An observed charge-off raises the posterior above break-even and switches the future action to decline. The fixed seed drew a performing pilot here and hence did not switch.

How often could an actual pilot have paid for its own charge-off in this episode? The April starter selected a loan in the April 30 batch, with 13 actual charge-offs among 982 funded loans. Neither May's nor June's first batch included a three-year charge-off. The chance of a pilot charge-off under the fixed design was therefore 13/(3 × 982) = 0.44128%. At follow-up, 83 of the 1,644 actual July 31 loans experienced a three-year charge-off. All 25 chronological slots came from that daily batch. With a performing margin of 1.7232 and loss of 50, avoiding at least two charge-offs among those 25 covers the pilot loss. For a uniformly sampled boundary batch, the ex-post probability of this path was (13/2946) × P[Hypergeometric(1644, 83, 25) ≥ 2] = **0.16005%**. This was the only one of the 16 selected episodes with positive probability of the strict success under those terms. The episode frequencies are conditional on observed historical outcomes.

Retain the originally sampled 25 future loans, which included three charge-offs. Replacing just the performing pilot with the first actually charged-off loan in the eligible archive-order batches gives an auditable retrospective example. The avoided continuation loss is **112.0905**, the pilot loses **50**, and the net is **+62.0905** under fixed scenario severity. Using each loan's recorded gross charge-off fraction, the same failed pilot loses **97.9968** per 100 and the same later portfolio loses **209.1327** if financed, so declining after that signal adds **+111.1359 net**. We identified this alternative pilot after examining allowable outcomes. Choosing it cannot be counted as an extra prospective success. The administrative charge-off fractions for this example concern observed nominal balances. The performing margin, exposures and lending decisions in both calculations belong to the replay. The calendar dates and repayments belong to the archive.

## Reproduction

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m paper11.pilot_transfer --out results --cache data/cache
PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m pytest -q tests/test_pilot_transfer.py
```

The source files weigh 146,535,485 and 318,696,252 bytes and are pinned by SHA-256. The two pricing scenarios use the same applicants and seed. The quarterly block bootstrap uses all 30 quarters as sampling units with regions kept together, including quarters without a selected pilot. The best-performing retrospective pilot is never used for scoring the precommitted draw.
