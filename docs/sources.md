# Data and provenance

* Assigned paper and online appendix: Kozodoi, Lessmann, Alamgir, Moreira-Matias & Papakonstantinou, [arXiv:2407.13009v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13009v1). Also published in [European Journal of Operational Research 324(2), 616–628](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0377221725000839). We compare against the assigned preprint. Authors' Spanish microloan data include a randomized approval holdout and were not supplied.
* Taiwan: I-Cheng Yeh, [Default of Credit Card Clients, UCI](https://doi.org/10.24432/C55S3H), CC BY 4.0. 30,000 financed credit accounts, 6,636 next-month defaults. X1 is credit limit; X6–11 are six past repayment statuses, X12–17 are bills and X18–23 are payments. The decision is a hypothetical renewal at the end of September 2005. All clients share one outcome month, so calendar extrapolation is tested separately on Prosper. KFold uses no outcomes.
* United States: [Udacity-hosted historical Prosper extract](https://s3.amazonaws.com/udacity-hosted-downloads/ud651/prosperLoanData.csv), [Prosper export documentation](https://www.prosper.com/Downloads/Services/Documentation/ProsperDataExport_Details.html). The March 2014 snapshot contains 113,937 entries. The unit in our test is the borrower's first funded loan observed in the extract. The endpoint is first *recorded default cycle* in 1–12, including subsequently recovered accounts. A default/charge-off with a missing or nonpositive cycle is excluded. Cancelled listings are excluded; no full-term status filter. Origination is the index date. Through February 2013 loans have at least twelve months of follow-up. This target differs from both next-month card default and Monedo's three consecutive months of delinquency.
* Historical approval is simulated for the Taiwan and Prosper cohorts. Raw files contain borrowers the original institutions already financed. Artificially rejected cases retain their historical observed outcomes as blinded ground truth for holdout evaluation and explicitly labeled retrospective diagnostics. Neither dataset identifies the effect of lending to actual rejects. No borrower-level data or identifiers are included in the repository.
* Input download URLs and SHA-256 fingerprints are in `src/paper11/data.py`. Downloads are atomic and verified on every run. The UCI archive also offers the same observations in XLS format. Observed-model features exclude identifier, protected demographics, and all post-decision events. Missing numerical predictors use fitting-cohort medians. Public results are aggregates.
* Philippines: Karlan and Zinman, Science (2011); public analytic file prepared by Athey, Fava, Karlan, Osman and Zinman. A separate score-window trial with actual offers and incomplete take-up. [Source, exchangeability, currency reconciliation and external reference counts](rct.md). The CSV is pinned to a commit and verified on download in `src/paper11/data.py`; only aggregate results are included.
